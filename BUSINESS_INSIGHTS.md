# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes daily sales data to automatically identify abnormal sales patterns and provide actionable business insights through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

Five anomaly detection methods were implemented:

- Sales Spike Detection
- Sales Drop Detection
- Zero Sales Detection
- Campaign Spike Detection
- High Volatility Detection

Each anomaly is automatically assigned a severity level and a human-readable explanation to help business users quickly understand potential issues.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Sales Spike

Sudden increases in sales may indicate:

- Successful marketing campaigns
- Seasonal demand
- Viral social media exposure
- Inventory replenishment

These events should be reviewed to identify successful business strategies.

---

### 2. Sales Drop

Significant decreases in sales may be caused by:

- Stock shortages
- Reduced customer demand
- Pricing changes
- Competitive promotions

Products with repeated sales drops should be investigated.

---

### 3. Campaign Spike

Campaign Spike detection identifies products experiencing unusually high sales during promotional periods.

These products can be used to evaluate marketing effectiveness and optimize future campaigns.

---

### 4. High Volatility

Products with highly unstable sales often indicate:

- Inconsistent demand
- Supply chain issues
- Promotional dependency

These products may require closer inventory planning.

---

## Dashboard Improvements

The dashboard provides interactive tools including:

- Date Range Filter
- Product Search
- Anomaly Type Filter
- KPI Summary Cards
- Top Products Ranking
- Interactive Charts

These features enable users to efficiently investigate abnormal sales behaviour.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor high-severity anomalies daily.
- Investigate recurring sales drops to reduce potential revenue loss.
- Analyze successful campaign spikes to improve future promotions.
- Optimize inventory for products with high volatility.
- Continue expanding anomaly detection with predictive analytics and forecasting models.
